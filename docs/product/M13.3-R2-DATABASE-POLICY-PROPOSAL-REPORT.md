Defence in depth: the worker asks for the attestation itself **and** the database decision now
withholds the address (`community_notice_missing`) when it is missing, so the worker never receives a
member as eligible when the database policy blocks them. WhatsApp and SMS are not evaluated by any
email function; the matrix keeps WhatsApp opt-in-only and SMS disabled. Payment-targeted topics are not
accepted by the collection decision function. Transactional eligibility
(`app.evaluate_email_eligibility`) is untouched.

## 6. Notification audience independence (follow-on phase N-AUD)

**Verified current state.** `app.resolve_collection_audience` (`20260920150000:250`) is the payment
audience: `all_members`, `groups` (`collection_audience_groups` → `member_group_memberships`), or
`selected_members` = members who already have an obligation. `public` resolves to nobody (the
announcement proposal special-cases it to all members). `collection_reminder_requests.notify_target`
is only `all`/`unpaid` (plus `update` in the proposal). The applied `app.publish_collection`
(`20261028100000`) and `app.send_collection_publish_reminder` (`20261029100000`) take no audience.
So "selected members" for a notification cannot be expressed without creating obligations — a
financial write — and R2 deliberately does not reuse payment eligibility for anything new.

**Why not in R2.** It changes two applied function signatures, the web actions and three dialogs.
A database-only half would be unusable, and any shortcut would change who is asked to pay.

**N-AUD database design (to propose after R2 is applied):**

1. `public.communication_requests` — one row per organiser send (publish announcement, reminder,
   update): `community_id`, `collection_id`, `kind`, `audience_mode`
   (`collection_audience` | `all_members` | `groups` | `selected_members`), `actor_user_id`,
   `idempotency_key`, `audit_log_id`. Either a new table or additive columns on
   `collection_reminder_requests` (decision).
2. `communication_request_groups (request_id, group_id)` and
   `communication_request_members (request_id, member_id)`, each with a same-community tenancy
   trigger (pattern `app.assert_audience_group_tenancy`), bounded size, deny-all RLS.
3. `app.resolve_notification_audience(request_id)` — current members of the community filtered by the
   request; `collection_audience` delegates to the payment resolver. **Never reads or writes
   obligations.**
4. `app.evaluate_communication_recipients(request_id, topic)` — the R2 decision function over the
   request's audience instead of the collection's; same order, same policy.
5. Additive audience parameters (with defaults that keep today's behaviour) on
   `app.publish_collection`, `app.send_collection_publish_reminder`, `app.announce_collection_update`
   and the preview; audit `after_state` carries the request id and counts, never member lists.
6. Worker: in-app and email both resolve through the request; templates get a variant for recipients
   outside the payment audience ("for your information", never implying an obligation).
7. pgTAP: cross-community groups/members refused; archived excluded; a member outside the payment
   audience is notified **and no obligation is created**; counts equal deliveries.

Dependencies: R2 applied (it revises the same decision function); G7 decision for members-only
collections (non-contributors have no page they can open); product decision on default audience and
on requiring `announcement.send` for non-default audiences.
