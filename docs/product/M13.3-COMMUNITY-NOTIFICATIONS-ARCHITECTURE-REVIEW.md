## 1. Product correction (what changes)

Community notifications are a core feature, not a marketing subscription. The previous design made
one member-level flag (`members.consent_messages_at`) a universal gate for every announcement and
reminder. Because nothing writes that flag, the effective behaviour is: **no member ever receives a
community email**. That contradicts the product and is replaced by a per-category, per-channel policy.

Note: `member_channel_consents` does not exist anywhere in the repository (no migration, proposal or
code references it). It appears only in the earlier plan. This review treats it as a rejected design
for a universal gate and reuses the idea only for the channels and categories that genuinely need
permission (WhatsApp, marketing).

## 3. Roles under the NDPA [LEGAL]

The legal basis question depends on who the controller is. Proposed reading:

- **The community (organiser) is the data controller** of its member roll: it decides who is a member,
  what contact data is held and why it is contacted.
- **Kolekto is the community's data processor** for community communications (categories A, B, D
  below), sending on the community's instructions under Kolekto's terms (which must contain the
  processor clauses the NDPA/GAID require).
- **Kolekto is a controller in its own right** for its own marketing (category C), platform security
  messages and platform accounts.

Consequence: Kolekto cannot "decide" a lawful basis on a community's behalf; it can require the
organiser to confirm one (attestation, §6.3) and build the product so that the confirmed basis is
respected.

## 6. Proposed architecture

### 6.1 Principles

1. **Membership ≠ messaging permission ≠ marketing subscription.** Three separate facts, three
   separate stores. Being on the roll never implies marketing consent; a marketing withdrawal never
   removes anyone from the roll or from category A.
2. **Policy is code, decisions are data.** The event → category map and the category × channel
   requirement matrix live in one reviewed module (worker) mirrored by one SQL function; a
   reclassification is a one-line reviewed change (existing Decision E principle, kept).
3. **Never fabricate permission.** Nothing back-fills a consent or opt-in. Default-on categories rely
   on a documented lawful basis, not on a synthetic consent row.
4. **Withdrawals win, at send time.** Every pending row is rechecked inside the claim transaction,
   immediately before the provider call.
5. **Every non-send is a `skipped` row with a stable code** (existing rule, kept).

### 6.2 Data model (additive; nothing dropped or renamed)

| Object | Purpose | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| `members` (existing) | The roll: who is a member, contact details. | **No change.** `consent_messages_at` is no longer read as a gate. It is not dropped and not back-filled; its column comment is updated to say it is legacy/unused (comment-only change). |
| Category policy (code + `app.communication_policy(event, channel)` SQL function) | Returns `category`, `requirement` (`none` / `opt_out_allowed` / `opt_in_required` / `blocked`) and `send_class`. | Single source of truth for processor, preview and send-time recheck. No table: policy changes are code-reviewed. |
| `member_communication_choices` (new, append-only) | History of every opt-out, re-opt-in and wrong-recipient report: `member_id`, `community_id`, `category` (`community` / `payment_reminder`), `channel` (`email` / `whatsapp` / `sms` / `all`), `choice` (`opt_out` / `opt_in`), `source` (`unsubscribe_link`, `member_self_service`, `organiser_on_member_request`, `whatsapp_stop`, `sms_stop`, `not_me`), `recorded_at`, `recorded_by`, `delivery_id` (if from a link). | Evidence that a withdrawal was respected (L8). Organiser can insert `opt_out` only; `opt_in` sources are member-only. Deny-all RLS; writes through definer functions. |
| `notification_preferences` (existing) | **Current-state projection** of the choices log, maintained by the same definer function in the same transaction. | Fits the four-category model with one clarification: `email_enabled` / `whatsapp_enabled` / `sms_enabled` apply to categories B and D2 only, never A (documented in the comment and enforced by the policy function). `announcements_enabled` + `collection_updates_enabled` = B; `reminders_enabled` = D2. **No new columns needed.** Gaps it cannot cover alone: history, source, and member-side writes — hence the log. |
| `member_channel_permissions` (new) | Opt-ins that a channel genuinely requires: WhatsApp (all categories), and any channel that counsel says needs one. `member_id`, `community_id`, `channel`, `granted_at`, `source`, `wording_version`, `withdrawn_at`. | This is the only place a "channel consent" survives — scoped to channels that need it, never a universal gate. |
| `marketing_consents` (new, later) | Category C only. Keyed by contact address + scope (`kolekto` or a community), `wording_version`, `consented_at`, `withdrawn_at`. | Not part of community notifications. Category C sends nothing until this exists. |

| Unsubscribe tokens | Stateless HMAC token over (`delivery_id`, `member_id`, `community_id`, `category`, `channel`), server secret, no expiry shorter than retention. | No table needed; the resulting choice is logged with `delivery_id`. |

### 6.3 How existing members become eligible (without fabricating consent)

1. No consent, opt-in or `consent_messages_at` value is ever written for existing members.
2. Category A: unchanged — already eligible today (E1/E2 rules stand, subject to L12).
3. Category B by email/SMS becomes eligible for a community when **both** hold:
   - the organiser has completed the community communication attestation (records the notice
     version, the officer and the time; audited), and
   - the member has not opted out (no choice row → default on, per the lawful basis).
   Until a community attests, B rows are written as `skipped / community_notice_missing`, and the
   publish dialog says why and links to the one-time attestation. This is a one-time community step,
   not a per-collection switch.
4. If counsel answers L3 "Kolekto must notify directly", the first category-B email to each member is
   preceded by (or is itself) an introduction notice: who the community is, why they are receiving it,
   how to stop. Tracked per member as `first_contact_notice_delivery_id` on the choices log
   (a notice, not a consent).
5. WhatsApp: existing members are **not** eligible until they opt in themselves
   (`member_channel_permissions`). No organiser import of opt-ins.
6. Marketing: no existing member is eligible. Ever, until they consent.

### 6.4 Eligibility decision (fan-out) — replaces the order in the proposal SQL

```
collection_not_reachable → member_unavailable →
  policy(category, channel):                      (communication-policy.ts decide())
    disabled               → skipped / payment_attribution_unavailable | channel_disabled | category_not_on_channel
    default_on (+notice)   → member opted out       → skipped / opted_out
                             community not attested → skipped / community_notice_missing
    opt_in                 → member opted out       → skipped / opted_out
                             no marketing consent   → skipped / marketing_consent_missing
                             no WhatsApp permission → skipped / channel_permission_missing
    always (essential)     → continue
→ address checks (unchanged: Q-FALLBACK, usable address)
→ address suppression (unchanged, at send)
```

As implemented in R1, the member's own opt-out is reported before the community-notice gap, so
organisers see the member's choice. `consent_missing` is removed from categories A, B and D;
consent survives only as `marketing_consent` for C. While the database proposal still applies the
retired gate (until R2), a member the policy would send to but the database withheld for consent is
filed as `legacy_consent_gate`.

### 6.5 Send-time recheck (new; required)

A new definer function `app.recheck_delivery_eligibility(delivery_id)` is called by
`app.reserve_email_attempt` (and the future WhatsApp/SMS equivalents) **inside the claim
transaction, before the rate-limit checks and the provider call**. It re-evaluates, against the
current database state:

| Check | Skip code |
|---|---|
| Member archived, merged or deleted | `member_unavailable` |
| Member's current address ≠ `recipient_address` (organiser edited the contact) | `recipient_changed` |
| Opt-out recorded since fan-out (category × channel) | `opted_out` |
| Channel permission withdrawn (WhatsApp) | `channel_permission_missing` |
| Community attestation revoked / community not active | `community_notice_missing` / `community_inactive` |
| Collection no longer reachable (unpublished, cancelled) | `collection_not_reachable` |
| Address now suppressed | `address_suppressed` (existing) |

Skips are policy outcomes, never retried as failures (existing rule).

### 6.6 Unsubscribe and member controls

- Every B and D2 email carries a one-click unsubscribe (`List-Unsubscribe` + `List-Unsubscribe-Post`)
  and a visible footer link: "Stop {community} collection announcements by email". A second link:
  "This isn't me" → removes the address from that member record via the choices log and flags it to
  the organiser.
- Category A emails carry "why you received this" and "this isn't me", but no unsubscribe.
- A members-without-accounts preferences page reached by token (no sign-in), showing per-category
  and per-channel choices for that community. Authenticated members get the same page in-app.
- Organisers see opt-out counts and can record an opt-out on a member's request; they can never
  reverse a member's opt-out or grant a WhatsApp/marketing permission.

### 6.7 Payment reminders (D)

Unchanged safety position: D2 stays `blocked` in the policy until payment attribution exists (G3:
contributions record tied to `obligation_id`, verified provider captures and officer-recorded offline
payments, derived per-obligation paid amount). Until then only B-class neutral reminders are offered,
and they never state or imply what the recipient owes.

## 10. Required capability: notification audience independent of payment audience

**Requirement.** An organiser must be able to announce a collection to **all community members**,
**selected members** or **specific groups**, independently of who may contribute to it.

**Current behaviour (verified).** There is no separate notification audience. Every collection
notification resolves recipients through `app.resolve_collection_audience`
(`20260920150000_collection_audience.sql:250`), which is the **payment** audience: `all_members`,
`groups` (via `collection_audience_groups`), or `selected_members` — defined as "members who already
have an obligation" ("naming someone and asking them to pay are the same act"). A `public`
collection resolves to nobody (the member-announcements proposal special-cases it to all members).
`collection_reminder_requests.notify_target` only distinguishes `all` / `unpaid`. So today an
announcement can never reach a member outside the payment audience, and "selected members" for a
notification cannot be expressed without creating obligations — which would be a financial write.

**Changes required (none started):**

| Layer | Change |
|---|---|
| Schema | Generalise the per-send request: a `communication_requests` table (or additive columns on `collection_reminder_requests`) with `audience_mode` (`collection_audience` / `all_members` / `groups` / `selected_members`), plus child tables `communication_request_groups (request_id, group_id)` and `communication_request_members (request_id, member_id)`, each with a same-community tenancy trigger (pattern: `app.assert_audience_group_tenancy`). No change to `collections.audience_mode`, `collection_audience_groups` or obligations: the payment audience is untouched. |
| Resolver | `app.resolve_notification_audience(request_id)`: current members (`active`/`unclaimed`/`invited`, not archived/merged) of the request's community, filtered by the request's mode; `collection_audience` delegates to `app.resolve_collection_audience` (and to all members for `public`). Never reads or writes obligations. |
| Eligibility | `app.evaluate_collection_email_recipients` gains a request-scoped variant (e.g. `app.evaluate_communication_recipients(request_id, topic)`) that uses the resolver above. Reachability unchanged: members outside the payment audience of a members-only collection still have no page they can open (G7). |
| API (DB + web) | `app.publish_collection` / `app.request_collection_reminder` / `app.announce_collection_update` accept `p_audience_mode`, `p_group_ids[]`, `p_member_ids[]` (additive parameters with defaults that preserve today's behaviour). Validation: groups and members belong to the community; bounded list size; permission `collection.publish` (and possibly the existing catalogue permission `announcement.send` for non-default audiences — product decision). Audit `after_state` carries the request id and counts, never member lists. Zod schemas in `packages/validation`, wrappers in `packages/db`. |
